Welcome to Think Outside the Chat™. This brief onboarding is designed to prepare you for your personalized AI transformation, and it serves as a helpful foundation rather than a formal training session. To get started, please complete the onboarding questions as honestly as you can. These responses establish your baseline, and we will begin your first session at our scheduled time.
