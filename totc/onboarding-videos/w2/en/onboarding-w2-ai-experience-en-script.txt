Before we begin your training, let's take a moment to look at your current AI experience. Think about how you are using these tools today, rather than how you plan to use them later. Consider the specific tasks you currently handle with AI. Whether it's research, writing, summarizing documents, or brainstorming new ideas, identify the uses that best reflect your actual day-to-day work. Next, reflect on your frequency of use. Establishing your current habits provides our baseline, so provide an honest estimate of how often you interact with AI tools to help guide your upcoming training. Your learning journey is personal. As you prepare to move from learning and implementing to delegating, these insights ensure your 10 hours of instructor-guided training are tailored to your needs.
